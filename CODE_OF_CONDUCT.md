# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want **LANSpeed** to be an open, welcoming, respectful, and constructive project for everyone.

Contributors, maintainers, issue reporters, reviewers, and community members are expected to participate in good faith and treat one another with respect.

The goal of this Code of Conduct is to provide a positive environment where people can:

* Share ideas.
* Ask questions.
* Discuss technical problems.
* Give and receive constructive feedback.
* Learn from one another.
* Contribute to the project without unnecessary hostility.

---

## 🌱 Expected Behavior

Examples of positive and respectful behavior include:

* Being respectful and considerate.
* Welcoming different viewpoints and experiences.
* Providing constructive technical feedback.
* Focusing criticism on ideas, implementations, and code rather than individuals.
* Accepting reasonable technical disagreement.
* Asking questions before making assumptions.
* Helping newcomers learn and contribute.
* Giving credit where it is due.
* Providing reproducible information when reporting technical problems.
* Respecting project maintainers and other contributors.
* Communicating professionally during disagreements.

Technical expertise is not a requirement for participation. Questions and beginner-level contributions are welcome.

---

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable within the LANSpeed community:

* Harassment or discrimination.
* Personal attacks, insults, or intimidation.
* Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
* Threats of violence or other forms of intimidation.
* Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
* Publishing private, personal, or sensitive information without permission.
* Deliberately disrupting project infrastructure.
* Malicious behavior toward contributors or maintainers.
* Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
* Deliberately submitting malicious or deceptive contributions.
* Encouraging unauthorized attacks against systems or networks.
* Using project spaces to facilitate harassment, abuse, or other harmful activity.

Disagreement is acceptable.

Abusive or hostile behavior is not.

---

## 💻 Technical Discussions

Technical disagreements are a normal and valuable part of open-source development.

When discussing implementations, contributors are encouraged to focus on objective technical considerations such as:

* Evidence
* Reproducible behavior
* Documentation
* Performance
* Security
* Maintainability
* Compatibility
* Reliability
* Project requirements
* Long-term technical impact

A contributor may strongly disagree with a proposal or implementation.

That disagreement should remain focused on the **technical subject**, rather than becoming a personal attack.

### Good Example

> "This approach could cause problems with partial TCP reads. We should use explicit framing here and add a test for fragmented messages."

### Not Acceptable

> "This implementation is stupid. You clearly don't understand networking."

Criticism should improve the project, not attack the person making the contribution.

---

## 🛡️ Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for helping maintain a healthy and productive project environment.

Maintainers may:

* Review and moderate contributions.
* Remove or edit inappropriate content.
* Close or lock discussions when necessary.
* Request changes to contributions.
* Temporarily restrict participation when appropriate.
* Take action against repeated or serious violations.

Maintainers should apply these expectations fairly and consistently while considering the context and severity of each situation.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, close, or reject contributions, comments, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, violations may result in:

1. A private or public warning.
2. Removal of inappropriate content.
3. Temporary restriction of participation.
4. Temporary suspension from project spaces.
5. Permanent removal from project participation.

Serious threats, harassment, malicious activity, or deliberate disruption may result in immediate action without a prior warning.

Enforcement decisions may consider:

* The severity of the behavior.
* Whether the behavior was intentional.
* Whether it was repeated.
* Its impact on other participants.
* Whether the person cooperated with maintainers.
* Any relevant context surrounding the incident.

---

## 📩 Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer privately through the project's available contact channels.

When reporting an incident, please provide as much relevant information as possible, including:

* What happened.
* When it happened.
* Where it happened.
* Who was involved.
* Links or screenshots when relevant.
* Any additional context that may help with the investigation.

Please avoid publicly posting sensitive details about an incident.

Reports will be reviewed as reasonably and confidentially as possible.

---

## 🔐 Privacy and Confidentiality

Information provided during a conduct report should be handled with appropriate discretion.

Sensitive personal information should not be publicly disclosed unless necessary and legally required.

Reporters should not be pressured to publicly discuss an incident.

Likewise, contributors should avoid publicly identifying individuals involved in an unresolved conduct matter.

---

## 🌍 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions within LANSpeed project spaces, including:

* GitHub repositories.
* Issues.
* Pull requests.
* Discussions.
* Code reviews.
* Project documentation.
* Community channels officially associated with LANSpeed.
* Other spaces where participants are representing or directly contributing to LANSpeed.

This Code of Conduct may also apply to conduct outside project spaces when it directly affects the safety or ability of project participants to contribute to LANSpeed.

---

## 🤝 Good-Faith Participation

LANSpeed is built by people with different levels of experience, backgrounds, and technical perspectives.

Contributors are encouraged to:

* Assume good faith when possible.
* Ask for clarification before making accusations.
* Explain technical concerns clearly.
* Accept constructive criticism.
* Correct mistakes when they are identified.
* Give others an opportunity to improve.
* Keep discussions focused on building a better project.

Nobody is expected to get everything right on the first attempt.

---

## 📜 Attribution

This Code of Conduct is intended to establish the behavioral expectations for the LANSpeed community and may be updated as the project evolves.

Project maintainers may revise this document when necessary to better protect contributors and maintain a healthy open-source environment.

---

## ❤️ Final Note

LANSpeed is an open-source project, and every contribution matters.

Whether you're submitting a major feature, fixing a small bug, improving documentation, reporting an issue, or simply asking a question, you're helping the project move forward.

Thank you for helping keep LANSpeed **respectful, constructive, and welcoming**. 🚀
